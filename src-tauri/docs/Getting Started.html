Welcome to MovieBox

Watch and download movies and series. Nothing else to install. Here's everything you need in three steps.

1

If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box

This appears the first time because MovieBox isn't sold in a store. It's safe to continue:

Click More info, then click Run anyway. You only do this once.

Illustration

Some antivirus programs may also ask once. Choose Allow.

2

Open MovieBox

Double-click the MovieBox icon on your desktop (or find it in the Start menu). A short welcome tour shows you around.

3

Find something and press Play

Use Search or browse Home. Click a poster, then press ▶ Play. It plays inside the app with subtitles.

Full screenPress F or double-click the video.
PausePress Space or click the video.
Skip← → skip 10 s. Hold Ctrl for 1 minute.
Volume↑ ↓ or the mouse wheel.
Watch offlinePress Download, then play it from Downloads.
All shortcutsPress ? while watching.

Something not working?

Open Help in MovieBox. It has a "Something's not working?" page with fixes in order, and a Report a problem button that opens the log file you can send to whoever helps you.

MovieBox is built on the open-source MovieBox-Tui and the mpv player. No accounts, no tracking.
